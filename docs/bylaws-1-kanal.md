# DHA Gujranwala bye-laws for a 1 kanal residential plot

Source: **DHA Gujranwala Construction & Development Regulations 2022 (Bye-Laws)** and the one-page *Summary of DHA GWA Regulation 2022*, both downloaded from dhagwa.com (copies in `sources/`). Clause numbers below refer to Part III of the regulations unless stated otherwise.

These are the rules as published in 2022. Confirm with DHA Gujranwala Building Control before submitting drawings, because rates and some clauses change by notification.

## Car porch

- One porch may rest on the boundary wall for a 1 kanal plot (two on a corner plot). 15(a)(10)
- Porch height ≥ 9 ft and ≤ ground-floor roof level. 11(b)
- Depth 12–18 ft (+2 ft projection) when the clear space is used for the porch. 15(a)(11)
- The porch roof must sit on its own columns (≤ 18″ × 24″ or 18″ dia.), not on the common wall; the roof may extend to the plot line. 15(a)(5), (7); 16(g)
- A folding fabric shelter may extend the porch by up to 6 ft. 15(a)(8)

## Boundary wall and gates

- Boundary wall ≤ **7 ft** above crown of the adjacent road; an electric fence up to 2 ft more is allowed with neighbour NOC; concertina wire is not. 16(a)(b)
- Main gate at the **left or right corner** of the front wall, ≤ **20 ft** including wicket (25 ft with extra charges). The gate must not be taller than the wall. 17(k), (o)
- Arch over the gate up to 12 ft from road level and 5 ft wide. 17(o)
- Guard post 6′ × 6′ × 8′ high, beside the gate on the lawn side. 18
- Outside the wall only a 2 ft flowerbed (≤ 6″ high) is allowed: no rockery, hedge, ramp-raising or water taps. 16(e), 27

## Clear spaces (setbacks): what may and may not go in them

Allowed: basement stair (2.5 ft wide), underground water tank, drains, septic tank, sewer line, 2 ft sunshades, benches/BBQ counters (taking at most half the width), rockery/water feature (with a 2.5 ft passage), one temporary 6′ × 4′ washing-machine shade. A spiral steel stair at the rear is allowed if there is no internal roof access. 17(b)(c)(i)

Not allowed: any building, servant quarter or guard room, pergola, geysers / water filters / washing area **in the side spaces**, dog or bird cages. 17(b)(f)(g)(h)

**At least two trees must be planted in the front lawn.** 17(e)

## Swimming pool

Allowed on plots of 4,500 sq ft and above, so a 1 kanal plot qualifies. 24
- In the basement, ground or first floor; **never on the roof**.
- Not in the clear spaces, except front or rear with case-by-case approval and 5 ft from the boundary wall.
- 10 ft privacy wall (or equivalent), change/shower room, filtration plant and holding tank are mandatory.
- DHA does not supply water for pools; separate charges apply.

## Roof

Allowed: water tank, AC units and ducts, stair tower, lift room, skylights, generator platform, solar (net metering recommended), parapet (mandatory if the roof is accessible, 3 ft). 19
Green roof terrace allowed for 1 kanal and above: plants ≤ 4 ft along the parapet for privacy, **no permanent seating, no sheds or pergolas**, a gazebo ≤ 100 sq ft on an engineered green roof. 20
Solar bonus: with a 10 kW+ system, half of up to 200 sq ft of excess covered area is not fined. 22(b)

## Water, sewer, fire

- RCC underground tank and an overhead tank are both required. Part IV-3
- **Grey-water tank required for 1 kanal and above** (for lawn and plants). Part IV-2(a)
- Water-efficient fixtures, targeting a 50% saving. Part IV-2(b)
- No bore, tube well or pump on the DHA line. Part IV-1
- RCC septic tank, ≥ 95 cu ft and ≥ 4 ft from the water tank, before the sewer connection; kitchen and bath water go to the septic overflow manhole. Part IV-5, 6
- Rainwater goes to open drains, not the sewer. Part IV-5(h)
- **Fire-fighting / fire-exit plan must be approved for houses of 1 kanal and above.** Part IV-9(b)

## Process and timeline

- Contractor must be PEC / PCATP registered and approved by DHA for 1 kanal and above; a PEC engineer supervises on site. 10(a)
- Soil investigation and SPT test by the DHA lab (paid). Summary sheet 5(b)
- Termite proofing before foundation RCC. 10(c)
- Start construction within 3 years of possession; finish within 2 years after that. Summary sheet 4
- DHA inspects at 9 stages; completion certificate within 3 years of approval. Summary sheet 7; 31(e)
- Fixed CCTV only, no PTZ cameras. 10(h)
