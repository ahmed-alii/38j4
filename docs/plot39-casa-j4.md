# Plot 39 · Casa J-4

A second design on plot 39 (directly north of plot 38, identical 1 kanal, west-facing). Geometry: `js/designs/plot39.js`. Check: `node tools/check-layout.mjs plot39` reports no problems.

## Brief (from the owner's references)

- **Look:** Mediterranean stone + stucco. Honey limestone ground floor and arches, warm white stucco above, dark iron windows and railings, green timber shutters, timber-lined eaves.
- **Smaller ground floor, bigger front and back:** 1,776 sq ft enclosed (Scheme A: about 2,400). The front lawn is 18′6″ deep beside the drawing-room bay and 24′6″ in front of the porch and parking (the minimum is 15′9″). At the back, an 8 ft arcaded loggia and then a pool garden.
- **No side courtyard.**
- **Wow spaces:** a double-height arched entrance hall with a lantern; arched colonnades between hall, dining and lounge; timber-beamed ceilings; an arcaded loggia; balconies over the front garden and the pool garden.
- **5 bedrooms, 4 baths + powder room** (fewer bathrooms than Scheme A).
- **Bedroom references:** in the master, the bed stands against a timber divider with an open his + hers dressing room behind it and the bath along one side; kids' rooms keep about 2′6″ around each bed.
- **Backyard references:** patterned encaustic-tile patio, timber pergola with bougainvillea over a 6-seat outdoor table, an outdoor kitchen counter, string lights and lanterns, tropical planting.

## Why rev 1 felt small, and what changed (rev 2)

Rev 1 had real-size furniture, but four things made it feel like a small house:

1. **The rooms were small for 1 kanal.** About 3,390 sq ft enclosed over two floors; most 1-kanal houses are 4,500 sq ft or more. Every room was 12 ft deep, the kids' rooms were 9′8″ and 11′ wide, and the drawing room was 13′ × 12′.
2. **Box furniture.** Every piece was a solid block down to the floor, so the floor disappeared under it. Real furniture stands on legs, has thin arms and open frames, and you see the floor under it.
3. **A narrow camera.** Walk-in mode used a 55° lens with the camera 3.4 m behind the avatar, which crowded rooms. It is now 72°, about what you take in standing in a room, with the camera over the right shoulder.
4. **Heavy ceilings and lines.** Beams were 9″ deep every 3′6″, and plot dimension lines were drawn through the walls. Beams are now 7″ deep every 4′6″, and dimension lines hide in walk-in mode.

Rev 2 fixes all four. It adds 158 sq ft without taking any of the back garden: a 6 ft bay at the front for the drawing room and bedroom 4. It opens dining and lounge into one 25′ × 25′ space, and rebalances the three front bedrooms upstairs.

To make it feel like a typical 1-kanal house, the next step is to give some garden back to the house. Up to 2,703 sq ft is allowed on the ground floor; rev 2 uses 2,090 sq ft.

## Realistic rendering (both designs)

- **Ambient occlusion:** ground-truth AO for soft contact shading in corners and under objects.
- **Bloom:** lamps and string lights glow.
- **SMAA anti-aliasing** and 4K sun shadows.
- **Sky photo:** a real cloudy sky (CC0, Poly Haven) as the background and for reflections.
- **PBR materials:** stone, stucco, terracotta, oak and parquet with relief and roughness maps.
- **Leaf cards:** trees, climbers, hedges and planting built from thousands of instanced, alpha-cut leaf cards that cast leaf-shaped shadows.
- **Furniture and glass:** rounded furniture edges, reflective glass and water.

"Fast" mode (default on phones) turns the post-processing off.
