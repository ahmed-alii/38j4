                  SUMMARY OF DHA GWA REGULATION 2022 (BYE-LAWS)

1.   Plot Sizes
            Type                  Residential                  Commercial
           2 Marla                      -                    15 feet x 30 feet
           4 Marla                      -                    30 feet x 30 feet
           5 Marla              25 feet x 45 feet                    -
           6 Marla              27 feet x 50 feet                    -
           8 Marla              30 feet x 60 feet            30 feet x 60 feet
          10 Marla              35 feet x 65 feet                    -
          20 Marla              50 feet x 90 feet            50 feet x 90 feet
          40 Marla             75 feet x 120 feet                    -
2.   Subdivision of Plots. Two Kanals Residential into 2 x 1 Kanal Plot
3.   Amalgamation of Plots
     a.    Residential. 2 x Plots can be amalgamated
     b.    Commercial Plots.
           (1)     3 x Corner Plots and 2 x Non-Corner Plots.
           (2)     More than 3 x Plots may be amalgamated, will be evaluated on case-to-case
                   basis.
4.   Construction period
     a.    Commence construction of the building within 3 years from the date of possession.
     b.    The person shall complete the building within a period of two years.
     c.    In case of violation, charges may be levied.
5.   General Construction Rules
     a.    For all residential plots 1 Kanal and above and all commercial plots, the contractor
           must be registered with PEC/ PCATP and approved by Authority.
     b.    Soil investigation & SPT test will be conducted by DHA Lab (with fees).
6.   Height Restrictions
     a.    Residential. Maximum height of residential building - 35 feet (from crown of road)
     b.    Commercial
           (1)     Sector shops. Ground, & First floor, height 24 feet.
           (2)     4 to 20 Marla Plots. Ground, mezzanine and Four Floors, height 72 feet.
           (3)     More than 20 Marla / JV/ BOT/ Special Projects. Allowable height will be
                   evaluated on case-to-case basis, shall not contravene the height regulations /
                   restrictions as set by the Civil Aviation Authority.
7.   Construction Inspection and Violations. During construction, DHA staff will visit site to
     ensure that construction is as per DHA bye-laws at 9 x stages.
